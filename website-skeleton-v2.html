Home · 03 · What we believe

Four things we hold to be true.

01

Wealth comes from ownership.

Nobody gets truly rich on a salary. Look at any rich list: it's people who built and owned businesses. A job pays your bills. Ownership buys your freedom.

02

There is no right age.

16 or 45, the market doesn't check your birth certificate. It checks whether someone will pay for what you made. You can start today.

03

You don't need a degree. You need the basics.

Marketing, strategy, finance: enough to get moving. That takes weeks, not years. The rest you learn on the business, while running it.

04

Your network is the moat.

Survival depends most on the people around your business: customers who trust you, founders who've seen your problem, one senior who picks up your call. AI can write your ads. It cannot build your relationships.

We help you become an entrepreneur. By making you build, not study.

Dhandha School is a 4-week cohort. You pick a track, you get live sessions with real Indian case studies, and every week you clear a checkpoint on a real business with your name on it. By Day 30 you haven't "learnt entrepreneurship". You've started.

One school. Two tracks. Pick yours.

The maker track

KARKHANA

You build a merchandise brand for a community you belong to: your college, your city, your fandom. Design, suppliers, pre-orders, margins. One rule is iron: you never print what nobody has paid for.

Capital: a few thousand ₹, mostly collected from customers first
The skills track

HUNARKHANA

India has over 6 crore small businesses, and most run on instinct. You pick a skill they need, package it as a service, price it, and land your first paying client. Sell to the shops around you.

Capital: ₹0

Sessions are combined across tracks. Checkpoints are separate. You learn together; you build alone.

Home · 10 · FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Who can apply?

Anyone from 16 to 45. Student, employee, in-between. We select on hunger, not marks. This is an application, not a checkout.

I don't have a business idea. Is that a problem?

No. The tracks exist so you don't need one. You need the willingness to talk to strangers and ask for money.

How much money do I need to start?

Hunarkhana: ₹0. Karkhana: a few thousand rupees, and the playbook makes your customers fund most of it through pre-orders.

How much time does it take?

Around 10 hours a week. 2-3 live sessions, the rest is fieldwork. The fieldwork is the course.

What if I miss a live session?

Recordings exist so you can revise, not so you can skip. Checkpoints don't move.

How is the Bangalore team picked?

Week 4 demo: real numbers, real customers, real story. The cohort watches, we decide, and we explain the choice publicly.

Do I get a certificate?

You get a P&L with your name on it. It says more.

4 facts · all public · all sourced · 2 minutes

29.1%

The more degrees, the more unemployment.

29.1% of Indian graduates are unemployed. Among those who never went to school: 3.4%.

India Employment Report 2024 · ILO + Institute for Human Development

The exam ladder gets more crowded at every rung, and the top rung doesn't pay.

−13%

AI is pulling up the ladder's bottom rung.

Stanford economists tracked millions of payroll records: employment for 22-25 year olds in AI-exposed jobs fell 13% after generative AI arrived. Older workers in the same jobs held steady.

"Canaries in the Coal Mine" · Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen · Stanford, 2025

The entry-level job you're preparing for may not exist by the time you're done preparing.

6.3 Cr+

India already runs on dhandha. Untrained dhandha.

India has over 6.3 crore MSMEs; Udyam registrations crossed 7.8 crore in Feb 2026. Almost all are first-generation owners with zero formal business training.

Ministry of MSME · PIB · Feb 2026

That's not a statistic. That's a customer list.

58.4%

The salaried job is the exception, not the rule.

58.4% of working Indians are self-employed. Only 21.7% earn a regular salary.

Periodic Labour Force Survey 2023-24 · MoSPI

Most of India already works for itself. Nobody teaches how to do it well.

The exam math  ·  JEE: ~15,00,000 candidates → 18,951 IIT seats  ·  CAT: ~3,50,000 → ~8,400 seats  ·  UPSC: ~10,00,000 → ~1,000 posts
